CONFIG-UNet: A Real-Time Deep Learning Framework for Low-Intensity Signal Restoration and Oxygen Saturation Mapping in LED-Based Photoacoustic Imaging
======================================================================================================================================================
Continuous and real-time monitoring of tissue oxygenation remains a critical unmet need in modern clinical care, particularly in contexts requiring spatially resolved oxygen saturation (StO2) mapping. LED-based photoacoustic imaging (PAI) offers a promising low-cost and safe alternative to laser-based systems, but its clinical translation has been hindered by the inherently low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), especially in low-intensity regions that are physiologically significant. We introduce CONFIG-UNet, a Conjoint Frequency & Intensity Regulated U-Net architecture, tailored to overcome these limitations through a novel deep learning approach that enables real-time denoising and restoration of low-intensity photoacoustic signals. The model integrates attention-dense-residual blocks, an intensity-aware loss that emphasizes low-SNR regions during training, and a frequency-domain regularization scheme that preserves spectral integrity essential for accurate StO2 estimation via dual-wavelength unmixing. Additionally, advanced perceptual quality metrics - Chaos-Aware Structural Similarity Metric and Turbulence-Weighted Image Quality - are employed to quantify reconstruction quality beyond conventional PSNR and SSIM. CONFIG-UNet demonstrates robust generalization across phantoms, murine tissues, and human extremity imaging, enabling accurate and dynamic mapping of StO2 under varying physiological conditions. Through comprehensive validation and interpretability analyses, this work establishes CONFIG-UNet as a framework for LED-PAI, manifesting real-time, high-fidelity oxygenation mapping that could reform monitoring in critical care, surgery, and beyond.
